# Practitioner-Driven Research

This skill is used when the user requests strategies, solutions, "how-to" advice, or wants to know "what people are saying" about a specific topic. It shifts the research methodology from theoretical/academic synthesis to real-world practitioner intelligence.

## Workflow
1. **Identify the Domain:** Determine if the topic is Software Engineering, AI/LLMs, Business, Marketing, etc.
2. **Targeted Search:** Instead of a general web search, use site-specific operators to find human-driven discussions:
   - `site:reddit.com [topic]`
   - `site:news.ycombinator.com [topic]`
   - `site:stackoverflow.com [topic]`
3. **Analyze for "Terrain Intelligence":** Look for:
   - **Hacks/Workarounds:** Unconventional but effective solutions used in production.
   - **Debates:** Areas where experts disagree (crucial for providing a nuanced view).
   - **Pitfalls:** Common mistakes and "lessons learned" reported by users.
4. **Synthesis:** Present findings as "The Reality of the Terrain" vs. "Standard/Theoretical Best Practices".

## Pitfalls
- **Do not** report only the most popular answer; report the debates.
- **Do not** ignore the "Why" behind a hack; explain the trade-offs.
- **Do not** mistake a single Reddit comment for a universal truth; frame it as "Community sentiment".
